How it works

Your chama, running itself.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does Chamaa work outside Kenya?

Yes. Chamaa works for savings groups in any country. Each chama has its own currency, and amounts are shown in your local currency. M-Pesa is available for Kenyan chamas; members elsewhere can pay by card or bank transfer.

Do all members need to download something?

No download is needed. Chamaa opens in any phone browser, and members can add it to their home screen to use it like an app.

Why do I need to verify my identity?

To keep everyone's money safe. Members verify with an ID, a selfie and their address before paying in, receiving payouts or adding a bank account. It takes about three minutes.

How do payouts work?

Your chama sets the payout order. Everyone can see whose turn is next, members can request a swap, and officials approve each payout before it's sent.

Can I keep personal goals too?

Yes. Alongside your group's shared goal, you can set personal goals, like school fees, rent or land, and watch them grow with every contribution.
